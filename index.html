GrowthHub Personal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What this app is

GrowthHub Personal is a private desktop tool used by a single creator to manage their own Instagram account: scheduling and publishing their own posts and reading the statistics of their own account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

  • The account owner's own Instagram and Facebook Page data (posts, captions, insights) needed to publish and measure posts.
  • Public information of other public Instagram professional accounts (follower counts and public posts), read through Meta's official Business Discovery API, for content inspiration only.

The app does not collect data from other users, does not log anyone else in, and does not sell, share or transfer data to third parties.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the owner's personal computer. Access tokens are stored in the Windows Credential Manager. No data is stored on external servers.

Data deletion

To remove the app's access, go to Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), select "GrowthHub Personal" and click Remove. All locally stored data is deleted when the app is uninstalled with the "delete data" option.
